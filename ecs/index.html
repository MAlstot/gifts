The AI Literacy Continuum

3

Mentor, not police

Four moves inside your own sphere of influence. Start with the diagnosis — it's the fastest, and it makes the other three concrete.

Start here · 4 minutes

Assignment Integrity Stress Test

Meet your assignment on your students' worst day, before they do. Seven bad-day students, built from the eleven reasons, run against work you already own. It diagnoses — it does not fix. What you change stays entirely your call.

Run the stress test

Policy · 8 minutes

Draft Your Disclosure Stance

Four questions, and you walk out with disclosure language in your own voice. Disclosure isn't compliance — it's the assessment. A student can't fake a reflection about thinking they never did.
